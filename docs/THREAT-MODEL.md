# Threat model

Last updated 2026-09-26. Status: **M** = mitigated and tested locally, **P** = partially mitigated,
**D** = designed (not yet built), **A** = accepted risk (documented).

## Assets

Customer conversations and contact data (per company); WhatsApp business tokens; payment references
and wallet balances; identity credentials and sessions; audit trails; platform administration power.

## Actors

Anonymous internet user · authenticated user of company A attacking company B · malicious or
compromised company member · compromised company owner account · malicious WhatsApp end user
(sends crafted messages/media) · forged provider (fake webhooks) · platform staff (insider) ·
supply-chain attacker · attacker with stolen database backup or read access.

## Trust boundaries

Browser ↔ web · Meta/Razorpay ↔ webhook endpoints · web/worker ↔ PostgreSQL (roles, RLS) ·
worker ↔ external APIs (egress) · platform area ↔ tenant area · secret manager ↔ processes.

| T26 | **Consent tampering or loss** (opt-outs overwritten by imports, reordered webhooks or members; history edited)                                       | Consent events are append-only (runtime role cannot update or delete; state written only by a definer trigger); later event wins with opt-out winning ties and the one-second provider-time window; imports never override an opt-out and need an attestation for opt-ins; webhook replays cannot record a keyword twice.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | M                                                            |
| T27 | **Incomplete erasure** (personal data left in messages, previews, status history, notes, identities or queued sends)                                 | One SECURITY DEFINER function erases every location in a transaction; status-history recipient ids can be cleared only inside it (guard trigger); integration test checks each table. | T28 | **Campaign sends after an opt-out, suppression, cancellation or pause** (stale audience snapshots, queued sends)                                     | Snapshots hold ids only; the runner re-checks erasure, number, suppression, consent, variables and the frequency cap with fresh data before queueing; the send worker re-checks consent, suppression, template approval and the campaign status under the send intent's row lock; pause/cancel cancel unclaimed intents in the same transaction as the status change. | T29 | **Runaway or duplicate campaign sending** (repeated ticks, concurrent campaigns, lost signals, replay after an outage)                               | One tick per campaign at a time (row lock, `SKIP LOCKED`) and per company (advisory lock); recipients move pending → queued once in the tick's transaction; early or duplicate signals are ignored; pacing, message cap, estimated spending cap and a deadline after which remaining recipients expire; sends reuse the Phase 2 idempotent pipeline (no automatic resend of unknown outcomes). 10,000-recipient test queues each recipient exactly once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | M                                                            |
| T30 | **Unauthorised campaigns** (a member sends to everyone, approves their own campaign, or keeps sending after leaving)                                 | `campaigns.write` + `campaigns.send` to submit; optional four-eyes approval by a different member with `campaigns.approve`; sends run under the submitter's authority and pause when that member leaves or loses `campaigns.send`; every transition is in the campaign history and audit log.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | M                                                            |
| T31 | **Misleading cost or results figures** (invented prices, unmeasurable metrics shown as numbers)                                                      | Prices only from platform-entered rate cards with a source URL, immutable once published; estimates labelled as upper bounds; rates shown with numerators and denominators from versioned metric definitions; clicks and conversions shown as not measured.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | M                                                            |
| T32 | **Reports leaking data or misleading** (another company's numbers, message content through a report, tampered response times, exports without trace) | Report queries run under the company's RLS scope in one read-only snapshot and return aggregates and member names only; reply turns are written only by triggers and cannot be updated or deleted by the runtime role; definitions persisted and versioned, unmeasurable metrics shown as unavailable, cost estimates list what they could not price; exports need `reports.export`, are formula-safe and audited before data is returned; isolation test for another company's report.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | M                                                            |
