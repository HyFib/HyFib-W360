# Data retention, export and deletion

Last updated 2026-09-27. Defaults below are **proposals** pending legal review for each launch market;
they are not a statement of legal compliance. Retention jobs are phase 5–7 unless marked built.

## Export

- Company export (owners, `tenant.export`, also while suspended, restricted or pending deletion)
  **[built, ADR-055]**: Settings → Company data streams one gzip-compressed JSON Lines file (a
  header, one line per row, an end line with the row counts) from one read-only, repeatable-read
  transaction. Every company table is exported with listed columns or excluded with a reason, and
  every column of an exported table is listed or omitted with a reason: credentials, secrets, token
  hashes, the registration PIN and Flow tokens are never exported (tests fail on an unclassified
  table or column). Members come with their name and email. Nothing is stored; one export runs at a
  time per company; each start is audited (`tenant.exported`) and emailed to owners and
  administrators.
- Contact export (`contacts.export`) **[built]**: streaming CSV. Cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`,
  `@`, tab or carriage return are prefixed with an apostrophe (formula injection), except validated
  E.164 numbers; every export is audited.

## Deletion semantics (distinguish clearly in UI and contracts)

Company deletion is built (ADR-055): an owner types the company address; the company is
`pending_deletion` for 14 days (works as suspended; any owner can cancel until the job starts);
then the worker deletes it. A platform owner can also schedule it (ADR-090), typing the address and
a reason the owners and administrators are emailed and read in the company: the same 14 days, the
same job and the same records kept; only a platform owner cancels such a deletion. The deletion
register keeps who scheduled and cancelled it and HyFib's reasons with the register's other
columns.

1. **Application deletion** **[built]:** the `tenant.delete` job deletes every row of the
   company's deletable tables (`ops.tenant_deletion_steps`, children before parents, a batch per
   transaction, resumable) and leaves a tombstone company row (status `deleted`, name "Deleted
   company", address `deleted-<id>`). Deleted: members and invitations, the company audit log,
   contacts and consent history, conversations and messages, templates, campaigns, automations,
   CRM, growth, Flows, commerce and store copies, API keys, webhooks, refund requests, queued
   emails. People's own
   accounts are not deleted (they may belong to other companies).
2. **Provider-side deletion** **[built]:** once per deletion, every outcome recorded: HyFib's app
   is unsubscribed from each WhatsApp Business Account (numbers stay registered in the business's
   account, and its templates and Flows stay there), HyFib's Shopify app is uninstalled, HyFib's
   WooCommerce webhooks are deleted (the store owner is asked to revoke the key, which the API
   cannot do), and HyFib's card token is deleted at Razorpay. Payment Links stay in the company's
   own Razorpay account; HyFib's Razorpay customer record is kept (no delete call is used).
3. **Backup expiry:** deleted data persists in encrypted backups until they expire (PITR window and
   backup retention, e.g. 7–35 days); after a restore, deletions completed after the restore point
   are applied again before access reopens (RUNBOOKS §8).
4. **Legally retained records** **[built]:** kept after deletion: the tombstone, the wallet,
   ledger, top-ups, holds, refunds of service credit (`app.credit_refunds`, including those
   approved from a company's request), card mandates (safe card metadata only),
   auto-recharge attempts, the
   Razorpay customer reference with its billing contact, plan history, plan subscriptions,
   plan payments and their refunds (`app.plan_payment_refunds`), the deletion register,
   support grants and security events, and the platform audit log. The legal basis and periods
   still need a tax and legal adviser's review.

## Contact erasure **[built]**

`app.erase_contact` (members with `contacts.delete`; allowed while a company is suspended) runs in
one transaction: cancels queued template and text sends, redacts every message in the contact's
conversations to `{"redacted": true}`, clears recipient ids in delivery history (the only change a
guard trigger allows, and only inside the function), deletes notes, media records, identities and
tags (with the notes' mentions), clears the contact's personal fields and appends marketing and
utility opt-outs. Consent history is kept as evidence. Reply turns, conversation cases and CSAT
survey scores stay (ids, timestamps and a 1 to 3 score, no content) so reports remain consistent;
the survey question and answer messages are redacted with the rest of the conversation. CRM: the
contact's timeline (with its notes) and tasks are deleted, and its deals keep stage, value, owner,
outcome and dates with the title replaced by "Deal (contact erased)" and the lost reason cleared. Growth: the
contact's conversation sources (which link or ad its conversations came from) are deleted and any
ad referral object stored on its messages before migration 0016 is cleared; link click counts
never identified anyone and stay. Flows: the contact's completed forms are deleted and the tokens
of forms sent to it stop working at once. Commerce: the customer's note is cleared from the contact's
orders (items and amounts stay as business records) and their open Payment Links are cancelled at
Razorpay by the worker. Store orders (Shopify, WooCommerce) with the contact's number or email lose
the customer's name, number, email, status page and tracking number and link for good (re-reading
an order never brings them back), order messages about them that are still waiting are not sent, and
when the erasure keeps the number suppressed, new orders from it are kept without them. Store
checkouts with the contact's number lose the customer's details, the recovery address and the
consent Shopify recorded for good, and cart reminders about them that are still waiting are not
sent. Automations started by order messages stop with the contact's other runs, and their order fields come back empty from redacted orders, so no later step sends the erased details. Media files are not stored yet; when media storage lands, erasure must
also delete the stored files. Data already sent to Meta is governed by Meta's terms (see deletion
semantics above).

## Consent withdrawal and suppression

Opt-out keywords, Meta's "user stopped marketing messages" error (131050) and manual suppression
stop future template sends immediately, including templates already queued and campaign
recipients in old audience snapshots: consent and the suppression list are re-checked when a
campaign queues a recipient and again right before dispatch **[built]**. Imports never override an
existing opt-out. Suppression entries are retained so the opt-out is honoured if the number is
re-imported or writes in again.
