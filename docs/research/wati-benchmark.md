# WATI (wati.io): Functional Capability Benchmark

- **Retrieval date:** 2026-09-26
- **Prepared for:** HyFib W360 product team. This is a functional benchmark only. No WATI text, screenshots or assets are reproduced, and every description below is paraphrased in our own words.
- **Method statement:** primary pages blocked by egress policy; findings derived from search-index results; must be re-verified by direct fetch before launch claims.

**Method details**

- Direct page fetches to wati.io, support.wati.io and docs.wati.io were refused by the session's network egress proxy. So were most third-party hosts tried: capterra.com, costbench.com, chatarmin.com, glama.ai, apps.shopify.com and wordpress.org.
- Evidence comes from about 70 web searches, mostly restricted to `wati.io`, `support.wati.io` and `docs.wati.io`. For each hit the search tool returns a title, a URL and a summary written by the search engine. We use those summaries as "official search snippet" evidence. They are **not** verbatim page text, and they can be stale, blended across pages, or wrong.
- `github.com` could be reached. We fetched WATI's own GitHub organisation (`wati-io`) and one third-party API-profile repository directly.
- The session's shared web-search budget (200 calls) ran out before a few planned queries. Those topics were rule-engine details, the WhatsApp Flows builder, Sequences, a QR-code tool and "Wati Shop". They are listed under "Unconfirmed / conflicting".
- WATI runs "GEO" content subdomains (`geo.wati.io`, `astra-geo.wati.io`). They showed up in results, but we did **not** use them as capability evidence.
- Prices, limits and plan gating change often. Treat every number as a point-in-time reading.

**Legend**

- **Tier codes:** G = Growth, P = Pro, B = Business, PAYG = Pay-as-you-go (India only). "All plans" means G, P and B.
- **Evidence type:**
  - *official search snippet*: a search result for a WATI-owned page (help center, docs, marketing site, blog, Astra help center or trust center).
  - *official search snippet (title-level)*: only the article title and URL confirm the capability, with little or no detail.
  - *official repo (direct fetch)*: fetched from github.com/wati-io.
  - *third-party source*: a site not run by WATI.
- **Confidence:**
  - **high**: a dedicated WATI help or docs article whose title and snippet agree.
  - **medium**: a single snippet, a marketing or blog page, title-only evidence, or a plan detail that may be stale.
  - **low**: the evidence conflicts or is only indirect.

## 3. Pricing & billing model notes

All wording is paraphrased. Each figure is labelled with where it came from. Prices are point-in-time readings.

**How the bill is built (official, WATI help-center snippets)**

- WATI bills three parts separately:
  - **Subscription.** Growth, Pro or Business, paid monthly, quarterly or yearly.
  - **Messaging charges.** Meta per-message fees, drawn from a prepaid wallet.
  - **Optional add-ons.**
- Included allowances per plan (G / P / B):
  - Seats: 3 / 5 / 5. Extra seats on P/B only.
  - Automation triggers per month: 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000.
  - Chatbots: 2 / unlimited / unlimited.
  - Teams: not found / 10 / 20.
  - API calls per month: Growth 10,000; P/B not found.
  - AI Co-pilot credits: included every month, but amounts are not confirmed.
- Trial: 7 days, no card needed, messages to the user's own number only. Existing customers can also trial a higher plan.
- PAYG (India only): a one-time ₹999, returned as messaging credit valid for 90 days. Marketing templates only; the snippet quotes roughly ₹2 per marketing message. 1 user, 1 chatbot, 1 keyword, up to 1,000 service messages a month, no API or integrations.

**Meta message charges and the wallet (official)**

- Since 1 Jul 2025, charges follow Meta's per-message model: each delivered template is priced by category (marketing, utility, authentication) and by the recipient's country code.
- Charges come out of a prepaid **credit wallet**.
  - Top-ups are by card enabled for international USD purchases.
  - Credits never expire, and a credit-usage history is available.
  - **Auto-recharge** exists: the customer sets the amount and the threshold that triggers it. It works for USD payments only.
- INR-denominated plans exist for India, for example the INR minimum balance for calling and PAYG pricing in rupees. A GCC regional pricing site exists; its currency is unconfirmed.
- WATI tells customers that from 1 Oct 2026 Meta will make free-form service replies billable per message, and will again charge for utility templates sent inside the service window. This needs checking against Meta.
- Whether WATI adds a markup on Meta message rates is **unconfirmed**.

**Calling (official)**

- Inbound WhatsApp calls are free.
- Outbound calls are charged per minute by Meta according to the destination, and deducted from the wallet.
- Minimum wallet balance: ₹500 on INR plans, $10 on USD plans.
- About $1 of free calling credit is given the first time calling is switched on.
- WATI says it adds no per-call markup.
- Recording, transcription and summaries are Pro/Business features that use AI credits: 3 per transcript, plus 1 for a summary.
- WATI calling-pricing content mentions 1,000 free minutes a month and then $0.01 per minute. It appears to refer to recording minutes; the scope is unverified.

**Add-ons (official unless stated)**

- Automation-trigger packs: billed on the base plan's cycle; price not captured.
- AI Co-pilot credit packs: prorated mid-cycle; price not captured.
- Extra seats: Pro/Business only; no official price captured.
- Custom domain: one-time $50 on Growth and Pro; included on Business.
- Dedicated hosting with a chosen region: $1,000/month billed annually; Business only.
- Extra WhatsApp numbers: Business only, up to 25 per account; price not captured.
- BYOA (bring your own AI agent): $100/month; Pro/Business.
- Shopify connection on Growth: $4.9/month, from one help-center snippet.
- Premium onboarding: price not captured.
- Legacy / possibly outdated: AI Support Agent responses at $30/month per 1,000; keyword actions at $100/month per 100 (pre-2025 plans).
